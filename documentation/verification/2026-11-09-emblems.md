## Not proven

- Clicking a realm in the menu opens that realm's board (pressing wired as in the expanded rail; not driven).
- Narrow widths, native builds, keyboard access to the menu, and the card hover.
- Whether eight tints and sixteen emblems keep realms apart past a dozen of them (they can collide).
