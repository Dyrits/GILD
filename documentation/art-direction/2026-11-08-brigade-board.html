Brigade — the kitchen pass

Direction G, with a new name. A professional kitchen is the best system people have built for running many jobs at once while one person keeps the whole picture: tickets come in, hang on a rail, cooks work them, someone calls when they need the chef, and the pass is where the chef checks each plate before it leaves. Brigade borrows all of it. Missions are thermal tickets on a steel rail; threads are the lines on the ticket; the docked mission panel is the pass under the heat lamps. Kitchen words stay in the decoration (CALL, the stamps, the pass), and the glossary's words stay on everything you act on.
