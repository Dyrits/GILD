TOWER — the strip board

Direction I, renamed Tower. Air traffic controllers keep a dozen aircraft safe at once with a tool older than radar: a paper strip per flight, slid into a colored holder, racked by what it needs next, and marked in grease pencil. Tower borrows the strip board. Each mission is a strip, racked into bays by state, with its holder color showing the state from across the room. The docked mission panel is the radio frequency you are talking on, and the scope in the corner shows every live mission at once. "Land" is already a Canopy word, so here it means exactly what it says.
