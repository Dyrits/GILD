Copydesk — the front page

Direction J, renamed Copydesk. A newsroom runs many reporters on many stories against one deadline, and an editor decides what runs. Copydesk borrows that. The board is today's front page, with missions laid out as stories by state. The lead story is the one you have open. Each thread is a reporter working the story. When an agent needs you, the editor's red pencil circles the story and writes "see me". The docked mission panel is the proof you read before it goes to press. Newspaper conventions do the work: headline weight carries priority, and column rules carry structure, so the page needs no cards, chips, or shadows.
