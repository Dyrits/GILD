Canopy — Forest Canopy Board

Direction F takes the other meaning of the name: the canopy of a forest, the layer of leaves above everything else. You work in its light. The rail is the dark green of the trees around you; the board is the forest floor with sunlight coming through in patches. Missions are leaves, with two rounded corners and two pointed ones. Running is new growth, Attention is a leaf turning orange, Done is a leaf that has dried and settled. The canopy itself shows up as the crown over the mission panel, the logo, and the empty state. Fraunces for headings and margin notes, Inter for everything you read.
