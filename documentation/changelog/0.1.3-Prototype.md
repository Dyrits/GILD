# Changelog: 0.1.3-Prototype

## CHG-0268 · 2026-11-14 · Harnesses and Accounts merged into one card per provider

Type: Code
Event: Agreement and Delivery
Scope: Provider accounts, Workshop

Summary: The owner asked to merge the Workshop's Harnesses and Accounts cards, keeping the page's title, and gave a second screen as a target to reach. The Providers section now has one card per harness: its logo, name, version, a Ready pill, "Update available · X" with **Update** (or **Install** when the harness is missing), and under a rule its accounts. An account's row shows its name, detail, a Signed in pill, **Rename**, and a "⋯" menu with **Sign in** or **Sign in again** and, for an added account, **Remove**; **+ Add account** at the foot opens its name field in place. The Computer name card stays above; **Refresh** sits with the one line of explanation. Choices of mine, for the owner to redirect: the target's left settings menu, the page title, and the "Connected" wording were not copied (the title was kept as asked, the pill says "Signed in" as before); Rename stays a link rather than a pencil; the account budgets stay on their own card in Limits.
Reason: Two cards listed the same three harnesses, so a harness's version, update, and sign-ins were read in two places.
