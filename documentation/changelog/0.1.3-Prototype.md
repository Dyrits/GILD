## CHG-0268 · 2026-11-14 · Harnesses and Accounts merged into one card per provider

Type: Code
Event: Agreement and Delivery
Scope: Provider accounts, Workshop

Reason: Two cards listed the same three harnesses, so a harness's version, update, and sign-ins were read in two places.
References: [provider-accounts specification](../capabilities/provider-accounts/specifications.md), [verification](../verification/2026-11-14-providers-merged.md), CHG-0265.

Driven in a throwaway Electron window on the fake harness (dark theme): the cards, Add account with a name, the "⋯" menu with and without Remove, Remove, and Rename. Not checked: Update and Install (the fake harness offers neither), a signed-out account, a long account detail such as OpenCode's three providers, a narrow window, the light and Gild looks, a paired device viewing a host's Providers, the phone build.

## CHG-0269 · 2026-11-14 · Unsent message text survives leaving a quest

Type: Fix
Event: Delivery
Scope: Message box (Composer)

Summary: Text typed in a quest's or Iskios's message box and not sent was lost when the owner went to another screen (such as the Workshop) and came back, because the box kept its text only in its own screen state. The box now keeps unsent text in memory under a name per box (`thread:<id>`, `iskios`, `handoff:<realm path>`) and restores it when the screen opens again; sending or emptying the box forgets it. Closing or reloading the window still forgets it, and a restored chip shows as its plain token. The side-quest form is unchanged.
Reason: A half-written request is work the owner should not have to retype after a look at settings.
References: `apps/app/src/ui/unsentText.ts`, `apps/app/src/ui/controls.tsx`, `apps/app/src/ui/chipEditor.web.tsx`.
Cause: Hypothesis confirmed by the window drive: the `Composer`'s `useState("")` is unmounted with its screen. No test seam renders the composer, so the regression guard is the window drive plus a unit test of the store.
Evidence: `documentation/verification/2026-11-14-composer-draft/drive.ts` on a throwaway fake-harness window: red before (the box was empty after Workshop and back), green after (text restored). Biome, typecheck, and the app tests pass (381 passed, 4 skipped). The look in a real use, native builds (the native editor keeps plain text), and the other composers were not driven.

## CHG-0270 · 2026-11-14 · Realm archive asks what happens to its quests and Rituals

Type: Code
Event: Agreement
Scope: Realm lifecycle

Summary: The realm archive confirmation lists the realm's active quests with a checkbox each, all unchecked. A checked quest moves with its whole tree to the Borderlands; the rest are archived with the realm. A quest at work cannot be checked, since a move never stops threads. The confirmation also names the realm's Rituals, which the archive switches off and keeps in the Rituals with a "Realm archived" notice; they stay off after Restore or Start afresh until the owner switches them on, and cannot be switched on or run while the realm is archived. A Ritual's quest is no longer counted or archived as an ordinary quest of the realm. Moving Rituals to the Borderlands is not offered.
Reason: Archiving a realm should not shelve work the owner still wants, and its Rituals need an explicit, visible policy rather than the incidental handling of their backing quests.
References: [Archiving and restoring a realm](../capabilities/mission-tracking/specifications.md#archiving-and-restoring-a-realm), [CHG-0271](#chg-0271--2026-11-13--realm-archive-recovery-and-the-realms-overview), [verification](../verification/2026-11-13-realm-archive.md).
Validation: The owner asked in the realm-removal thread that the confirmation inform them and let them choose with checkboxes which quests move to the Borderlands, and that it ask for confirmation about Rituals. Running quests, an unchecked default, and the Ritual notice were my choices within that request; the owner may change them at review.

## CHG-0271 · 2026-11-13 · Realm archive, recovery, and the Realms overview

Type: Documentation
Event: Agreement
Scope: Realm lifecycle and navigation

Summary: The owner agreed to archive realms rather than delete them, through the realm panel or the top-right archive button, with a counted confirmation and an offer to stop running threads. The Archive offers realm restoration, preserving prior individual quest archives. Successful quest archives return to their realm or the Borderlands; successful realm archives return to a new compact Realms overview grouped by realm. Reusing an archived realm's folder offers Restore, Start afresh, or Cancel, preserving the realm id and folder uniqueness. Start afresh leaves every previous quest archived and deletes only realm-level lore through the existing recoverable deletion/history. Both recovery paths offer independent old/proposed name and crest choices in a two-column popup, skipping equal rows or an entirely equal popup. Restore defaults to old values and Start afresh to proposed values. A blank form name resolves to the folder name; the shuffled starting crest is a real proposed value. Start afresh ends with a separate counted wipe confirmation that explains recovery through the Deleted filter. Duplicate active names are allowed with a confirmation warning and parent-folder hints where applicable. Right-click actions remain deferred.
Reason: Removing a realm must not lose its quest history, lore, or workspace folder. Explicit folder reuse avoids silently selecting an archived realm, and independent name/crest choices let the owner keep either version.
References: [Mission-tracking behavior and acceptance](../capabilities/mission-tracking/specifications.md#archiving-and-restoring-a-realm), [Realms overview](../capabilities/mission-tracking/specifications.md#realms-overview), [workspace lifecycle](../capabilities/workspace-attachment/specifications.md#hiding-and-deleting-a-workspace), [lore history and undo](../capabilities/scoped-intel/specifications.md#history-and-undo), [implementation plan and status](../work-in-progress.md), [verification and review](../verification/2026-11-13-realm-archive.md).
Validation: The owner's confirmations of Q1–Q10 and amendments in the realm-removal thread, including the top-right archive action, Start afresh, independent name/crest selection, folder-name fallback, and the shuffled initial crest. The owner subsequently authorized the implementation plan with "Go for it."
Evidence: Existing folder reuse and lore deletion/history were inspected in the code. No implementation, executable verification, or visual acceptance is claimed.
