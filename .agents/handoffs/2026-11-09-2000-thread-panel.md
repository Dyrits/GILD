## Decisions (the agent's, for the owner to confirm)

- The panel shows what the thread's header showed plus the gauge, in a 300px column, closed at first, remembered in browser storage, absent below 820px.
- The gauge's parts: Instructions, Lore, Journal, Messages (counted at about 4 characters per token) and Tools and results (the remainder); counted parts that exceed the reported total are scaled down so the sum is exact.
- Orange from 80%, red from 95% of the window; the window is 200,000 tokens for every model, as the lore gauge counts it.
- The budget window stays apart from the gauge; skills are not a gauge part (threads do not load them).
