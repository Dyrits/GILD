## Decisions

- Rituals are not quests in any list: the owner asked for them to be separate with a menu like Quests'. A Ritual's runs still happen in its own quest's main thread, opened from its card or rail row. Recorded in the [rituals specification](../../documentation/capabilities/rituals/specifications.md) and CHG-0100.
- A Ritual quest cannot be marked done or archived from its panel (archiving would hide it from every list); a Ritual is switched off or deleted, and deleting keeps the quest as an ordinary one. Agent's choice, provisional.
- Rituals are listed latest first; no folding of long lists beyond the rail's own category fold.
- The agent did not know what the owner disliked in the first look; the new style (violet dashed cards, compact form) is a guess, to be corrected by the owner.
- Pushing to `main` passes the guard with `git push --no-verify`; the owner approved this push in their message.
