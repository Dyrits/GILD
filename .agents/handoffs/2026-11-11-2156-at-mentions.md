## Goal

Typing `@` in a message box opens a menu like Delta's and OpenBot's: categories first, then a filtered list. The owner chose three categories: Quests (with side-quests), Realms, and Files & Directories. A mention is context only: the receiving thread decides from the prompt whether to inspect the item or message that quest. Done when the owner has seen it and accepted the look and the behavior.

## Decisions

- Categories are Quests, Realms, Files & Directories only; no Terminals, Users, or "Ask about Iskios" (owner, this session; recorded in CHG-0181).
- A mention is context the thread interprets, resolved by the service at delivery so the state is current; the owner's text stays as typed (CHG-0181).
- A quest the owner mentions opens reading its journal and messaging its top thread for that turn only; a realm mention opens nothing; only the owner's messages open access (CHG-0181).
- Archived quests are excluded (owner).
- Builder's choices awaiting the owner: the token form; a thread receiving a message from another quest may reply in that turn (slightly wider than the agreement).
