## Decisions

- Provisional choices, listed in work-in-progress (Batch 15). The weightiest:
  - Undo puts back the fields the change changed (text, kind, scope, pin, origin) whatever happened since; the last use goes back only when no use came after; uses are never taken back.
  - Fading steps have no Undo; Restore covers them. They are worked out with the current half-lives.
  - A change awaiting approval enters the history only once approved; a declined one never does.
  - Intel saved before 0019 starts its history with one "Saved" entry.
- Structure:
  - `intelHistory.ts` records: snapshots, entries, `trackChange` (wraps a change with its before and after), fading-step storage, and erasing a deleted thread's history.
  - `intelHistoryViews.ts` reads: an intel's history (with steps not stored yet) and deleted intel.
  - `intelUndo.ts` undoes an entry.
  - `fadingStepsOf` in `intelFading.ts` works out when an intel crossed 0.15 and 0.05.
- Data: migration `0019_intel_history` adds `intel_history`, each entry with `before_state` and `after_state` as JSON. A fading step's identifier is `<intel>:<Dormant|Archived>:<time>`, so a step shown before it is stored keeps its identifier.
- Contract: requests `ReadIntelHistory` and `UndoIntelChange`; `IntelStateFilter` gains `Deleted`; `IntelView` gains `deleted`. Thread tools did not change.
- Drive tip: to see fading steps in the window, move an intel's `saved_at`, `updated_at`, `last_used_at`, and its `intel_history.at` back together; moving `last_used_at` alone puts the steps before the save.
