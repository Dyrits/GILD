## Decisions

- Threads keep their harness sessions across a move; the journal entry tells the thread. A first version forgot the session and replayed the conversation, on a belief the probe disproved. Recorded in CHG-0172.
- Only dragging initiates a move, as agreed (CHG-0133); a menu or keyboard way, a move by the Guildmaster, and native drag are not built.
- A realm tied to no folder is a realm row whose threads have no workspace (ADR 0015), so the specification's "removes workspace attachments" holds through it.
